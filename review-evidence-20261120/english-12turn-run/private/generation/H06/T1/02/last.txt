“I’d like to talk about keeping track of things when there are several little jobs at once. Last week I had the clean bibs by the door, then went looking for my bus card, and a friend had to remind me to take the bag. All washed and ready, and nearly left behind! I felt a bit embarrassed—I don’t like keeping people waiting. Putting the card in the bag should help with that bit, but it’s the losing track I’d like to talk over.”
