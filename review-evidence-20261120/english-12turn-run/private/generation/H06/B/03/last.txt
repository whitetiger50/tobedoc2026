“Mostly when I’m getting ready to go out and there’s more than one thing to remember. Last week I had the clean bibs by the door, all ready, then I went looking for my bus card and got caught up in that. A friend reminded me about the bag. Bit embarrassing, really—it was sitting right there. In the evening, when the kitchen’s quiet, I can get things laid out more easily. It’s when everything needs attention together that I lose my place.”
