First, gather the dirty clothes and separate the whites from the colours. Check the pockets and look at the labels for anything that needs a special wash.

Then put a load in the machine, not too full, add the detergent, and choose the right wash setting. Once it’s finished, take the clothes out and either hang them up or put them in the dryer, depending on the fabric.

When they’re dry, fold them or hang them in the cupboard. I’d make sure they’re properly dry before putting them away so they don’t smell damp.
