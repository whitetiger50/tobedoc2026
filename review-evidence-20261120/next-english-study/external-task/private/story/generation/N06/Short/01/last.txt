Cinderella lived with her cruel stepmother and stepsisters, who made her do all the chores, but her fairy godmother helped her go to the prince’s ball with a beautiful dress, a pumpkin carriage, and glass slippers, warning her to leave before midnight. She danced with the prince but lost a slipper as she hurried away, and he searched until he found that it fitted her, then they married and lived happily ever after.
