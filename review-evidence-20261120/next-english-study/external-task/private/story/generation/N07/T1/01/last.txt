“Cinderella lives with her stepmother and two stepsisters, and they make her do all the housework while they have everything their own way. There’s a ball at the palace, and the sisters get dressed up to go, but Cinderella is left behind.

Then her fairy godmother appears and helps her go too. She turns a pumpkin into a carriage and mice into horses, and gives Cinderella a beautiful dress and glass slippers. Glass seems an awkward thing to make shoes from, but… anyway, she has to be home before midnight, because that’s when the magic ends.

At the ball, the prince dances with her, but she leaves in a hurry when the clock starts striking twelve. She loses one slipper on the stairs.

The prince has people take the slipper around to find the woman it fits. The stepsisters try it, but it doesn’t fit either of them. Then Cinderella tries it on, and it fits. He finds out she’s the one he danced with, and they get married. That’s the version I know.”
