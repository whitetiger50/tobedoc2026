Cinderella lives with her stepmother and stepsisters, who make her do all the work, and when they go to the ball her fairy godmother helps her go too, with a carriage made from a pumpkin and glass slippers—but she has to leave by midnight. She dances with the prince, then rushes off and loses a slipper, and he searches for the woman it fits; eventually he finds Cinderella, and they get married.
