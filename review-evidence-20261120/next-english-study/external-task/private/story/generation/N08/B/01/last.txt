Cinderella lived with her stepmother and two stepsisters, who treated her badly and made her do all the housework. One day, they were invited to a ball at the palace, but Cinderella was left behind.

Her fairy godmother appeared and helped her go. She turned a pumpkin into a carriage and gave Cinderella a beautiful dress and glass slippers. But she warned her that the magic would end at midnight.

At the ball, Cinderella danced with the prince. When the clock began striking twelve, she hurried away and lost one of her glass slippers on the stairs.

The prince searched for her, asking people to try on the slipper. Her stepsisters couldn’t fit into it, but Cinderella could. He recognized her, and they got married and lived happily ever after. That’s the version I remember, anyway.
