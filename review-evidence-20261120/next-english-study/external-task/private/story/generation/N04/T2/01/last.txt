Oh, Cinderella. Well, she lives with her stepmother and two stepsisters, and they make her do all the housework. They’re very unkind to her. There’s a ball at the palace, and the sisters get dressed up to go, but Cinderella gets left behind.

Then her fairy godmother appears. She turns a pumpkin into a coach, and mice into horses, and gives Cinderella a beautiful dress and glass slippers. Imagine trying to walk in those—I’d be afraid of breaking one. Anyway, she tells her she must be home by midnight, because that’s when the magic wears off.

At the ball, the prince dances with Cinderella and falls in love with her. But then the clock starts striking twelve, and she runs off before she can tell him who she is. She loses one of her slippers on the stairs.

The prince has people take the slipper around to find the woman it fits. The stepsisters try it, but it doesn’t fit either of them. Then Cinderella tries it on, and of course it fits perfectly. So he finds her, and they get married and live happily ever after. That’s the version I remember.
