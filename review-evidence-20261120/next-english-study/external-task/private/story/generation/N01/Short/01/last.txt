Cinderella lives with her cruel stepmother and stepsisters, who make her do all the chores, but her fairy godmother helps her go to the prince’s ball with a dress, a pumpkin carriage, and glass slippers—though the magic only lasts until midnight. She dances with the prince, rushes off and loses a slipper, and he eventually finds her because it fits, so they marry and she gets away from all that housework.
