Sure. Cinderella lives with her stepmother and two stepsisters, who treat her badly and make her do all the housework. There’s a royal ball, and they go off to it while she’s left at home.

Her fairy godmother appears and gives her a lovely dress, glass slippers, and a carriage made from a pumpkin. The catch is she has to leave by midnight, when the magic wears off. Always a catch.

At the ball, she dances with the prince, but when the clock starts striking twelve, she runs off and loses one of her slippers. The prince searches for her by having women try it on. The stepsisters can’t get it to fit, but Cinderella can. He recognizes her, they get married, and they live happily ever after.
